# Thai payment QR formats

A Thai merchant can display one QR for several payment methods. A concrete example is Siam Commercial Bank's published combined QR. Its raw payload contains a Visa-reserved field at root tag `02` and a domestic PromptPay bill-payment template at root tag `30`. The amount in tag `54` is `100.00`, and the final CRC is `3EA1`. A scanner that stops at the first merchant account misses the bill-payment option. [SCB QR generation, “QR 30 & QR CS” response](https://developer.scb/assets/documents/api-reference-index/qr-payments/post-qrcode-create.html)

## Reading the merchant accounts

| Root tag | What to look for | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `02`, `03` | Both IDs are reserved for Visa. Preserve their values as opaque strings. An account field is enough to identify the allocation. The payment operator defines its contents. | [HKMA Common QR Code Specification, §4.2, table 4.2A](https://www.hkma.gov.hk/media/eng/doc/key-functions/finanical-infrastructure/infrastructure/retail-payment-initiatives/Common_QR_Code_Specification.pdf) |
| `26`–`51` | These are templates. Subtag `00` identifies the operator. Check that identifier before interpreting the other subtags. Local assignments differ across markets. | [HKMA, §4.2, table 4.2B](https://www.hkma.gov.hk/media/eng/doc/key-functions/finanical-infrastructure/infrastructure/retail-payment-initiatives/Common_QR_Code_Specification.pdf), [Bank of Thailand, §2.3](https://www.bot.or.th/content/dam/bot/fipcs/documents/FPG/2562/EngPDF/25620084.pdf) |
| `29` | The merchant-presented PromptPay transfer identifier is `A000000677010111`. The proxy can be a mobile number, national or tax ID, or e-wallet ID. The published table reserves bank-account subtag `04` for future use. | [Bank of Thailand, §2.3](https://www.bot.or.th/content/dam/bot/documents/th/our-roles/payment-systems/about-payment-systems/ThaiQRCode_Payment_Standard.pdf) |
| `30` | Domestic bill payment uses `A000000677010112`. The standard specifies a 15-digit biller ID, a mandatory first reference and an optional second reference. Upstream PromptParse also has a compatibility test with a 13-digit biller ID. For integration, use the identifier assigned by the biller's provider. | [Bank of Thailand, §2.3](https://www.bot.or.th/content/dam/bot/fipcs/documents/FPG/2562/EngPDF/25620084.pdf), [PromptParse test cases](https://github.com/maythiwat/promptparse/blob/main/src/index.test.ts) |

## Where Visa generation belongs

SCB's merchant API can issue a card-scheme QR (`CS`) or a combined card and bill-payment QR (`PPCS`). It requires a merchant profile and an access token, and returns the payload in `qrRawData`. A Thai merchant integration can obtain that string from the acquirer and use a local parser to inspect all advertised accounts. Preserve the original issued payload for display. Visa describes the subsequent payment as an authenticated issuer request processed by the acquirer. [SCB QR generation](https://developer.scb/assets/documents/api-reference-index/qr-payments/post-qrcode-create.html), [Visa Direct, MerchantPushPayments](https://developer.visa.com/api-reference/visa_direct)

For the Rust port, the defensible offline extension is recognizing the Visa-reserved fields alongside PromptPay and exposing their original values. A reader can test it against SCB's single-scheme and combined samples. A Visa merchant acceptance integration would also need an acquiring partner and a way to receive confirmation or inquire about payment status. [SCB Thai QR payment flow](https://developer.scb/assets/documents/documentation/qr-payment/thai-qr.html)

## Other related formats

- **Cross-border Thai QR.** The Bank of Thailand assigns a separate bill-payment identifier, `A000000677012006`, to overseas merchants. It also specifies the merchant country and additional currency and amount information in `62.08`. Changing the domestic identifier alone would omit required data. Regional QR links depend on participating institutions, including the Thailand–Indonesia QRIS linkage. [Thai QR payment guideline, §2.3](https://www.bot.or.th/content/dam/bot/fipcs/documents/FPG/2562/EngPDF/25620084.pdf), [Bank of Thailand cross-border payment arrangements](https://www.bot.or.th/en/financial-innovation/digital-finance/digital-payment/cross-border-payment.html)
- **Receipt mini QR.** SCB documents service ID `000001`, the sending bank and the transaction reference. The receipt parser can extract those lookup keys and check the payload checksum. Confirming payment requires a bank response. [SCB mini QR field layout](https://developer.scb/assets/documents/documentation/qr-payment/extracting-data-from-mini-qr.pdf), [SCB slip verification](https://developer.scb/assets/documents/api-reference-index/qr-payments/get-billpayment-transactions.html)
- **Consumer-presented payments.** EMVCo publishes separate consumer- and merchant-presented specifications. In SCB's MyPromptQR flow the customer generates a code, the merchant scans it and the bank processes the debit. Supporting that flow would require its own decoder, fixtures and bank integration. [EMVCo QR specifications](https://www.emvco.com/emv-technologies/qr-codes/), [SCB MyPromptQR flow](https://developer.scb/assets/documents/documentation/qr-payment/thai-qr.html)
